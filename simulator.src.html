Tree records are the project's 10,000-row sample dataset, used unchanged. Terrain, surrounding canopy and land cover come from public layers (Copernicus GLO-30 elevation, Meta/WRI 1 m canopy height, ESA WorldCover 2021, Hansen tree cover). Neighbouring trees, weather, flight physics, imagery, LiDAR and multispectral readings are simulated. No real drone flew over agarwood here.
